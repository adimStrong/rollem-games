        Swerte Lights Terms & Conditions

        1. Open to verified TamasaPH players aged 21 and above. Self-excluded, restricted or unverified accounts are not eligible.
        2. Every ₱200 of verified deposit made between 12:00 PM and 9:00 PM gives 1 play (for example ₱400 = 2 plays), up to 5 plays per player per day. Your plays are on the personal link from your agent; your deposits are checked automatically from your account. Pending, failed or reversed deposits do not qualify.
        3. Each play unlocks only once your turnover for the day reaches 1× the deposit for that play (₱200 deposit → ₱200 in bets, ₱400 → ₱400, and so on). Bets on any game count. Turnover is read from your account and refreshes every few minutes.
        4. The promo runs daily: deposits count from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM, and the plays on your link must be used by 11:59 PM the same day — your link expires at midnight and cannot be used the next day. Total bonus paid by this promo is capped at ₱15,000 per day for all players; once the cap is reached, new plays wait for the next day.
        5. Each play's bonus is set at random by the system the moment you press START: ₱8.20 or ₱16.50. In Swerte Lights the light runs around the board and stops on a symbol of your prize group. On some plays the light lands on WILD and then hits every symbol of one group once; the hits add up to the same prizes. Where the light stops makes no difference to the amount. The ₱777 max jackpot (77), BAR BAR (₱77) and the ₱777 wild tease are display-only and can never be won or collected. This is an animated promo game only, not a bet.
        6. Results are final once you press START. Refreshing the page, reopening the link or using another device does not give extra plays or a new bonus; an unclaimed play is shown again as it was.
        7. Every win is recorded automatically. The bonus is credited the following day at 1:00 PM after verification and must be wagered 1× (1× wagering) before it can be withdrawn.
        8. One claim per play. Multiple or linked accounts or any attempt to abuse this promo void the bonus and may lead to action on the account.
        9. TamasaPH may change, suspend or end this promo at any time. TamasaPH management's decision on any dispute is final.
        10. Play responsibly. Set limits on your deposits and time, and ask for help if gambling stops being fun.
