    Terms & Conditions

    1. Open to verified Time2Bet players aged 21 and above. Self-excluded, restricted or unverified accounts are not eligible.

    3. Every ₱200 of one verified qualifying deposit gives 1 run (for example, ₱600 = 3 runs). The minimum deposit is ₱200.
    4. In each run you pick one of six horses before the race, and your horse's finishing place sets your prize. Prizes are random in every run, and which horse you pick makes no difference.
    5. The promo runs daily from 2 PM to 9 PM. Total promo bonus is capped at ₱10,000 per day; once the cap is reached, the promo closes for the day.

    8. Only bets on slot games are valid for this promo. The bonus must be wagered 1× (Wager ×1) on slot games before it can be withdrawn.
    9. One claim per player per promo cycle. Multiple or linked accounts, edited links, or any attempt to abuse this promo void the bonus and may lead to action on the account.
    10. Time2Bet may change, suspend or end this promo at any time. Time2Bet management's decision on any dispute is final.
    11. Play responsibly. Set limits on your deposits and time, and ask for help if gambling stops being fun.
