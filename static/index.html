        Pearl Mine Terms & Conditions

        1. Open to verified POTS players aged 21 and above. Self-excluded, restricted or unverified accounts are not eligible.
        2. Every ₱150 of verified deposit made between 12 PM and 9 PM gives 1 play (for example ₱300 = 2 plays), up to 5 plays per player per day. Your plays are on the personal link from your agent; your deposits are checked automatically from your account. Pending, failed or reversed deposits do not qualify.
        3. Only bets on slot games count for this promo. Each play unlocks only once your slot turnover for the day reaches 1× the deposit for that play (₱150 deposit → ₱150 in slot bets, ₱300 → ₱300, and so on). Turnover is read from your account and refreshes every few minutes.
        4. The promo runs daily from 12 PM to 9 PM. Total bonus paid by this promo is capped at ₱10,000 per day for all players; once the cap is reached, new plays wait for the next day.
        5. Each play's bonus is set at random by the system the moment you press START. In Pearl Mine you open clams one by one: the pearls you find add up to your bonus. A sea urchin ends the round and you keep the pearls already found. If you press COLLECT before all the pearls of your bonus are found, the tide brings you the rest, so your total is the same either way. Which clams you open makes no difference. The prize ladder (₱7 · ₱17 · ₱27 · ₱57) shows your running total. The ₱27 and ₱57 tiers and the black ₱77 pearl are display tiers only and can never be won or collected. The pearls shown in the unopened clams at the end of a round — including the ₱777 pearls — are for display only and are not added to your bonus.
        6. Results are final once you press START. Refreshing the page, reopening the link or using another device does not give extra plays or a new bonus; an unfinished round continues where you left it.
        7. Every win is recorded automatically. The bonus is credited the following day at 1:00 PM after verification and must be wagered 1× (1× wagering) on slot games before it can be withdrawn.
        8. One claim per play. Multiple or linked accounts or any attempt to abuse this promo void the bonus and may lead to action on the account.
        9. POTS may change, suspend or end this promo at any time. POTS management's decision on any dispute is final.
        10. Play responsibly. Set limits on your deposits and time, and ask for help if gambling stops being fun.

        By ticking the agreement box and playing, you accept these Terms & Conditions.
