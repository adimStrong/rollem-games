        ROLL IT Terms & Conditions

        1. Open to verified Roll'em players aged 21 and above. Self-excluded, restricted or unverified accounts are not eligible.
        2. One deposit of ₱200 or more made between 12:00 PM and 9:00 PM gives 1 roll — a bigger deposit is still 1 roll, and two ₱300 deposits are 2 rolls, up to 5 rolls per player per day. Your rolls are on the personal link from your agent; your deposits are checked automatically from your account. Pending, failed or reversed deposits do not qualify.
        3. Each roll unlocks only once your turnover for the day reaches 1× the deposit for that roll (₱200 deposit → ₱200 in bets, ₱400 → ₱400, and so on). Bets on any game count. Turnover is read from your account and refreshes every few minutes.
        4. The promo runs daily: deposits count from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM, and the rolls on your link must be used by 11:59 PM the same day — your link expires at midnight and cannot be used the next day. Total bonus paid by this promo is capped at ₱10,000 per day for all players; once the cap is reached, new rolls wait for the next day.
        5. Each roll's bonus is set at random by the system the moment you press Roll: ₱7.50, ₱17.65 or ₱27 (the top prize, the rarest result). The 3 dice then land on a total that matches it: 3–11 for ₱7.50, 12–16 for ₱17.65, 17–18 for ₱27. Prizes up to ₱777 may be offered during special promos. This is an animated promo game only, not a bet.
        6. Results are final once you press Roll. Refreshing the page, reopening the link or using another device does not give extra rolls or a new bonus; an unclaimed roll is shown again as it was.
        7. Every win is recorded automatically. The bonus is credited the following day at 1:00 PM after verification and must be wagered 1× (1× wagering) before it can be withdrawn.
        8. One claim per roll. Multiple or linked accounts or any attempt to abuse this promo void the bonus and may lead to action on the account.
        9. Roll'em may change, suspend or end this promo at any time. Roll'em management's decision on any dispute is final.
        10. Play responsibly. Set limits on your deposits and time, and ask for help if gambling stops being fun.
